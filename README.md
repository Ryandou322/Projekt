# A portfolio of selected professional projects in Product Management, GIS, Data Visualization, and B2B systems.

## B2B Employee Benefits Mall – Quota & Account System

Designed the end-to-end quota management flow for a B2B employee benefits platform, covering the process from enterprise contract setup to employee spending.

The solution connected enterprise contracts, company funding pools, dedicated malls, employee quota codes, account binding, and order deduction. Key functions included allocating company budgets to specific malls, configuring products and pricing, generating employee quota codes, binding quotas to employee accounts, and synchronizing used and remaining balances across the system.

This project focused on improving data consistency and operational efficiency across enterprise funding, mall configuration, employee access, and transaction management.

My role: Product Manager – requirements analysis, workflow design, functional design, stakeholder coordination, and delivery follow-up.

## Smart Water Utility GIS Visualization

Designed GIS-based visualization solutions for a municipal water utility, integrating spatial data with operational information such as pipeline networks, inspection tasks, dispatching, customer service, and equipment status.

The project included map-based monitoring, thematic visualization, dashboard design, and 2D/3D presentation of key operational data. The goal was to help users understand network conditions, locate issues quickly, and support daily dispatching and management decisions through a unified visual interface.

My role: GIS Project Manager / Implementation Consultant – requirements analysis, visualization design, prototyping, stakeholder communication, implementation coordination, testing, and project delivery.
